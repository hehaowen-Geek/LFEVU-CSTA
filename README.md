# 漯河食品工程职业大学网络空间安全技术协会

## 关于我们

**LFEVU-CSTA-NovaSec**
（漯河食品工程职业大学网络空间安全技术协会）
*Cybersecurity Technology Association · NovaSec*

LFEVU-CSTA-NovaSec 是一个以**网络空间安全技术**为核心的学生技术组织，致力于打造集**学习、实践、竞赛与技术交流**于一体的专业化社区。

协会聚焦 **网络安全、CTF 竞赛、网络工程、系统运维、漏洞分析与安全防护** 等方向，强调理论与实践相结合，倡导“以技术为本、以实践为导向、以能力为目标”的成长理念。

我们通过技术分享、项目实践、竞赛训练与文档沉淀，不断提升成员的专业能力，并逐步构建结构化、可持续传承的技术知识体系。本知识库正是协会技术积累与经验沉淀的重要载体，为成员的学习、研究与协作提供支持。

**NovaSec** 不仅是一个名称，更代表我们对技术探索、安全守护与持续成长的共同追求。
欢迎每一位热爱技术、勇于探索的同学加入我们，一起在网络空间中学习、实践与创造。

**LFEVU-CSTA-NovaSec**
(Cybersecurity Technology Association of Luohe Food Engineering Vocational University)

LFEVU-CSTA-NovaSec is a student-led technical organization dedicated to **cybersecurity and network technologies**. We aim to build a professional community that integrates **learning, hands-on practice, competitions, and technical exchange**.

Our association focuses on areas such as **cybersecurity, CTF competitions, network engineering, system administration, vulnerability analysis, and security operations**. We emphasize the combination of theory and practice, encouraging members to grow through real-world problem solving and continuous technical exploration.

Through technical sharing sessions, project-based learning, competition training, and knowledge documentation, we continuously improve members’ skills while building a structured and sustainable knowledge system. This knowledge base serves as a central platform for preserving technical experience and supporting collaborative learning within the association.

**NovaSec** represents more than a name — it reflects our shared vision of exploration, security awareness, and long-term technical growth.
We welcome all students who are passionate about technology to join us and explore the world of cybersecurity together.
